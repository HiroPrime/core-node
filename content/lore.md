# The Nexus — Universe Lore

Everything below is true. No invented facts. This is the world the Core Node site lives in,
and the language the site should speak.

---

## The premise

The Nexus is not a portfolio. It is a machine that builds itself, one Node at a time,
and a person at the center of it who mostly talks.

She does not sit down and type a spec. She talks, the machine listens, and the page
comes out looking like the thing she already had in her head. That is the whole method.
Everything else — the circuits, the Nodes, the Queue — is just the machinery that made
that possible, given names so it feels like a place instead of a folder.

**The Core is her.** Core Node is not a company. It is the one surface that is allowed
to be only her.

---

## The Grid

A flat dark plane of live circuit traces. Signal runs the traces. Where a trace thickens
and glows, something got built, and that something is a **Node** — a project that grew
into its own world. Every Node keeps its own gravity, its own color, its own work. The
Core holds the map.

Connections between Nodes are not decoration. They are the actual wiring of the house:
what talks to what, what feeds what, what is still benched in the dark.

---

## The Operator

**BasicHiro** is the pen name on public work. **Charlie** and **Jacob** are the same
person and both are true. She is non-binary. She is a solo developer. She is a product
designer first and a developer second, and she is proud that she codes by talking.

Speech in, products out. She talks more than she types. Every word she has ever sent
this machine started as her voice.

---

## The Nodes

### Core Node — the Core
The central hub. Where the Grid is mapped and where the Operator sits. Home of the
press-start screen, the Node select, and the Stack. This world has no product. It has
a person.

### Save Point — the Identity Studio
Where a Character gets made. You get a First Look — cinematic on top, T-pose underneath
— and from that one body every later page is cut. Sheets, Rerolls, one page at a time,
her grade before anything moves. Qwen paints it. Haiku writes the words. Both jobs are
different and neither one does the other's work.

It runs on the Omen, the GPU box, with the watcher awake and Comfy on **8188** only.
Nothing else is allowed to start that forge.

**Free until it Works.** Then: Break the Server. Flood the Queue. Put her to work.

### Prime Portal — the Observatory
TCG analytics. Pokemon, search, and the analytics beast. A deck that reads itself.
Recharts is the scrying glass. This is the other half of the desk, and it is live as a
product, which is not the same as done.

### Grimm Fracture — the dark world
AI generative web comic. **Benched.** The files stay on the shelf. The Node is cold and
it is not on the desk. It is not gone, it is resting.

### Nexus Prime — the holding bay
Quiet. Left standing. Not the desk.

## Voice rules (for anything written on the site)

- Capitalize the words that matter: Free, Server, Queue, Character, First Look, Sheet,
  Reroll, Launch, Break, Core, Node, Grid, Universe.
